# STUHI app — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 22 August 2026**

This page explains what the STUHI app does with your information. It is written to
be readable, not to be impressive. If anything here is unclear, email
**support@stuhi.org** and we will explain it properly.

## In short

- We keep your **name and email** so we can let you into the event and give you food.
- If you choose to fill in a **profile** (a short bio and the roles you pick), other
  people at the same event can see it. That part is optional and you can hide or
  delete it whenever you want.
- We **do not track you**, we show **no ads**, we use **no analytics**, and we do
  **not sell or share** your information with anyone outside STUHI and the small
  number of services we need to run the app.
- You can **delete your account and everything in it from inside the app**:
  Profile → Settings → Delete account.
- The app is for people aged **13 and over**.

## 3. Your event registration (the Luma form)

If you signed up for a STUHI event using our public registration page (hosted by
Luma), we take **your name and email address** from that registration and transfer
them into the STUHI app so that we can issue you a personal admission QR code. The
registration page tells you this at the moment you register.

Some detail, because it matters:

- This is the **same information you gave us yourself**. We do not obtain your
  details from anywhere else. We do not buy, scrape, or receive personal data about
  you from third parties, and we never look you up in any other database.
- We import **only your name and email**. Anything else the registration form
  collects stays out of the app.
- We store your email in the app's registration list as a **scrambled (hashed)
  value**, not as readable text, so that the app can recognise you when you sign up
  without holding your address.
- **A registration does not create a profile.** Nobody can see you in the app until
  you sign up yourself and accept the Terms of Use. There are no hidden or
  pre-created profiles.
- If you register but never install the app, your registration row is **deleted 14
  days after the event ends**. You can also ask us to delete it sooner — email
  support@stuhi.org.

## 5. Who can see what

**Other participants at the event can see:** your display name, your bio, and the
roles you picked — but only if you chose to publish your profile, and only if they
are themselves confirmed participants of the same event. Your profile is never
public on the web, never visible to search engines, and never visible to someone who
has not been confirmed for the event.

**Other participants cannot see:** your email address, when you checked in, whether
you have eaten, who you blocked, or anything you reported.

**STUHI staff running the event can see:** your name, whether you have been admitted,
and whether you have collected a meal. Named STUHI moderators can also see content
you have posted and reports about it.

**Nobody else sees anything.**

## 7. How to change your mind, and how to delete everything

### Hide your profile
Profile → Settings → **Hide my profile**. Your bio and roles stop being visible to
other participants immediately, and you are taken out of team matching. This is how
you withdraw the consent in section 4 — it is exactly as easy as giving it was.

### Clear your own content
You can clear your bio, edit or delete your team's description if you own it, and
withdraw a join request you sent — all from inside the app, all taking effect
straight away.

### Delete your whole account
**Profile → Settings → Delete account.** Two taps and a confirmation. You do not
need to email anyone, call anyone, or fill in a web form. Deletion happens
immediately.

When you delete your account, we delete:

- your account and email address,
- your display name, bio, roles and challenge interests,
- your team memberships (the team itself stays for the other members),
- every join request and invitation you sent or received, including copies showing
  on other people's screens,
- your push notification token,
- your admission QR code, which stops working straight away,
- reports you filed.

**Two things do not simply disappear, and you should know about both:**

1. **Door and meal records.** We keep the fact that "somebody was admitted at 09:14
   and collected lunch at 12:31", but we scramble it so it can no longer be
   connected to you. **30 days after the event we destroy the key that made that
   scrambling possible**, together with the event registration list, at which point
   these records are permanently anonymous and are not personal data any more. We
   keep them so our catering and safety headcounts stay correct.
2. **If your account was removed for breaking the rules** (harassment, hate speech,
   threats and so on), we keep a **scrambled version of your email address only** —
   no name, no bio, nothing readable — purely so the same person cannot immediately
   sign up again during the event. This is based on our legitimate interest in
   keeping other participants safe (Article 6(1)(f)). **We delete this list entirely
   on 30 November 2026.** This does not apply if you simply delete your own account.

Everything else is gone.

## 8. Reporting, blocking and moderation

The app has a bio and team descriptions that people write themselves, so it also has
safety tools:

- **A filter.** Bios, display names and team descriptions are checked before they
  appear. Offensive language, and email addresses, phone numbers and social media
  handles, are rejected with an explanation. The check runs on our server, not just
  on your phone.
- **Report.** Every profile, every team and every incoming request has a Report
  action. Reports go to a named STUHI moderator who is on rota during the event, and
  we aim to act within **24 hours**.
- **Block.** Every profile and team has a Block action. A blocked person disappears
  from your lists, cannot send you anything, and cannot see you. They are not told
  that you blocked them. You can unblock from Settings → Blocked people.
- **Contact.** moderation@stuhi.org, always, for anything the in-app tools do not
  cover.

Moderators can read and remove anything posted in the app. When we remove something
you posted, we will tell you what was removed and why. Records of reports and the
action we took are kept for **12 months** and then deleted.

## 9. If you are under 18

A lot of the people using this app are 15 to 19 years old, so this section is for you.

- **You must be at least 13 to use the STUHI app.** In Finland, the Data Protection
  Act (1050/2018, § 5) says that from the age of 13 you can agree to services like
  this yourself, without a parent. You confirm at sign-up that you are 13 or older.
  If you are under 13, please do not create an account — and if you already have,
  email support@stuhi.org and we will delete it.
- **We do not ask for your date of birth.** We do not want it. A yes/no answer is
  enough for the law, so that is all we collect.
- **Your bio is optional.** You never have to write anything about yourself. You can
  be placed on a team using only the role tags you pick. If you would rather write
  nothing at all, that is completely fine and nothing in the app will be closed off
  to you.
- **Nobody outside the event can see you.** Your profile is only ever visible to
  other confirmed participants at the same event, and only while the event is
  running. It is not on the web and it cannot be found by searching.
- **There is no private messaging in this app**, no swiping, no ratings, no rankings,
  no scores, no "most popular" list and no profile photos. We left all of that out
  on purpose.
- **You never have to give a contact detail to another user**, and the app will
  actively stop you from putting an email address, phone number or Instagram/Snapchat
  handle in your bio. Please keep it that way. If an adult or anyone else asks you to
  move a conversation off the app, report it: moderation@stuhi.org.
- **If something feels wrong, tell someone.** Use Report in the app, or email
  moderation@stuhi.org, or find any STUHI organiser at the event. You will not get
  in trouble for reporting something.
- If you are under 18 and your parent or guardian wants to ask about your data, they
  can email support@stuhi.org and we will help — though the rights below are yours,
  not theirs.

## 11. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the following rights. To use any of them, email
**support@stuhi.org**. We answer within 30 days and it is free.

- **Access (Art. 15)** — ask what we hold about you, and get a copy.
- **Correction (Art. 16)** — fix anything wrong. You can already edit your name, bio
  and roles yourself in the app.
- **Deletion (Art. 17)** — delete everything. The **Delete account** button in the
  app does this immediately; you do not have to email us at all.
- **Restriction (Art. 18)** — ask us to stop using your data while something is being
  sorted out. **Hide my profile** covers the everyday version of this.
- **Portability (Art. 20)** — get your data in a machine-readable file.
- **Objection (Art. 21)** — object to the things we do on the "legitimate interest"
  basis listed in section 4.
- **Withdraw consent (Art. 7(3))** — turn off the published profile at any time,
  with one switch, in Settings.
- **Complain (Art. 77)** — if you think we have got this wrong, you can complain to
  the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (Tietosuojavaltuutetun toimisto),
  **tietosuoja.fi**. We would much rather you told us first, but it is your right.

**Automatic team placement.** If you are still without a team near the end of
matching, an organiser can run an automatic placement that puts you on a team. A
human organiser can change any placement — just ask. Nothing about this decision is
final, automatic or unreviewable.

## 12. Keeping your data safe

- Everything between the app and our servers travels over **HTTPS/TLS**. There are no
  exceptions and no unencrypted connections.
- Data is stored **encrypted at rest** in the European Union.
- Every table has **row-level access rules**, so the database itself refuses to hand
  your data to someone who should not have it. Hiding something in the app's
  interface is not how we do access control.
- **Admission QR codes are signed on our server.** The signing key never leaves the
  server and is not inside the app, so nobody can forge an entry pass by taking the
  app apart. Tokens are short-lived and are checked against what they are being used
  for — a meal scan cannot silently become a door admission.
- Your sign-in session is stored in the **iOS Keychain** on your phone, not in
  ordinary app storage.
- If there is ever a data breach that puts you at risk, we will tell the Finnish Data
  Protection Ombudsman within **72 hours** and tell you directly if the risk to you
  is high.

## 13. Where your data is

Your data is stored in the **European Union**. Push notifications are delivered
through Apple's servers, which may process the notification outside the EU under
Apple's standard contractual clauses. Nothing else leaves the EU.

Push notifications never contain anything private: no email addresses, no bios, no
details about another person. A notification says "Food is being served in Hall A" or
"You've been placed on Team 12", and nothing more sensitive than that.
